Before we begin

Your questions,
answered.

Who is Sandsprings built to support?

Foreclosure and creditor rights law firms, mortgage servicing companies, title and settlement teams, and individual or small private investors seeking operational support for U.S. mortgage files and real estate transactions.

Do you support both residential and commercial closings?

Yes. Closing support covers residential and commercial transaction workflows. We agree on the specific tasks, documentation, and review responsibilities for each engagement.

Can we start with one workflow?

Yes. We can discuss a defined initial scope, such as referral setup, title curative support, or closing document preparation, before considering a broader engagement.

How are access and confidentiality handled?

Access, confidentiality requirements, and permitted handling of information are agreed during onboarding. Ask us about working within your approved systems and remote-access environment. Please do not send borrower records or confidential case documents through this website.

Does Sandsprings provide legal advice?

No. Our role is operational and document support under client instructions. Your attorneys and authorized professionals retain responsibility for legal advice, legal decisions, and final approvals.
